Key Findings · Distant Reading

Five data-driven findings comparing Andersen's Fairy Tales, Arabian Nights, and Grimms' Fairy Tales across authority, interiority, signature vocabulary, family roles, and the natural world.

Finding 1
Power words: who governs each tradition
The single most diagnostic word per corpus: king dominates Grimms at 13× the rate it appears in Andersen, while Arabian Nights revolves around the sultan — a term absent from the other two.
Andersen Arabian Nights Grimms
Andersen: king 28, sultan 0, emperor 12. Arabian Nights: king 171, sultan 349, emperor 3. Grimms: king 368, sultan 0, emperor 8.
Finding 2
Interiority vs. action: heart, death, and gold
Grimms and Andersen use heart far more than Arabian Nights — suggesting a European emphasis on feeling and interiority. Arabian Nights leads heavily on gold and death, signalling a tradition driven by material stakes and consequence.
Andersen Arabian Nights Grimms
Heart: Andersen 51, Arabian 28, Grimms 73. Death: Andersen 29, Arabian 78, Grimms 26. Gold: Andersen 22, Arabian 110, Grimms 89.
Finding 3
Signature words: what each corpus owns exclusively
Some words are so concentrated in one corpus they function as fingerprints. Shadow is almost entirely Andersen's (85 vs. 0/0). Lamp belongs to Arabian Nights (90 vs. 5/2). Forest is Grimms' domain.

Finding 4
The shared feminine: mother, princess, and the family axis
Mother and princess are the two most cross-traditional terms. Father skews heavily Grimms — the paternal authority figure is a Grimms archetype, not an Andersen one. Arabian Nights gives the most weight to the princess as a narrative object.
Andersen Arabian Nights Grimms
Princess: Andersen 84, Arabian 290, Grimms 103. Mother: Andersen 61, Arabian 158, Grimms 154. Father: Andersen 15, Arabian 82, Grimms 183.
Finding 5
The natural world: sea, water, bird
Arabian Nights is a maritime tradition — sea appears 4× more than in Andersen. Bird is Grimms' most frequent nature word, embedded in tales like The Golden Bird. Andersen, a coastal Dane, is surprisingly restrained on the sea — his water imagery is more intimate.
Andersen Arabian Nights Grimms
Sea: Andersen 13, Arabian 110, Grimms 31. Water: Andersen 43, Arabian 73, Grimms 113. Bird: Andersen 16, Arabian 71, Grimms 121.
