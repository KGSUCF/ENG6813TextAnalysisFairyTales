3. The Moral Framework Matrix

Structural pathways linking functional behaviors directly to universal systems of rewards and dynamic consequences.

Brothers Grimm: Contractual Morality
● Good Path Triggers

Compliance with guide advice, hospitality to poor strangers, showing kindness to woodland animals.

● Bad Path Triggers

Vanity/Pride, active physical cruelty, violating explicit taboos or opening forbidden doors.

■ Structural Result

Absolute Physical Justice: The good inherit stable earthy kingdoms; the wicked face immediate, brutal physical punishments.

Arabian Nights: Transactional & Cosmic
● Good Path Triggers

Generosity, unyielding hospitality, respecting the bond of salt, deployment of wit and sharp intellect (Aql).

● Bad Path Triggers

Betrayal of fraternal bonds, economic fraud or severe covetousness, proud hubris against fate or God.

■ Structural Result

Restoration of Honor & Fortune: Dynamic return to financial abundance and high political standing aligned with divine destiny.

Hans Christian Andersen: Pietistic Spiritual
● Good Path Triggers

Absolute self-sacrificial love, preserving pure childlike innocence, speaking unvarnished plain truth.

● Bad Path Triggers

Superficial vanity, cynical worldviews, cold calculations leading to an explicit loss of empathy.

■ Structural Result

Spiritual Transcendence: Resolution decoupled from earth rewards. Protagonists may perish physically but achieve eternal grace.
